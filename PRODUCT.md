# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Marketing teams at consumer brands and the agencies that run several brands for them. The first customer is Bois & Chiffons, a French solid-wood furniture shop: a small team (social media manager, designer, an approver, a commercial owner) working in French, on laptops during the working day. They are marketers, not engineers; they want the tool to understand their brand quickly and then help them publish better content with less effort, without losing control.

## Product Purpose

Prow PLUS AI CMO is an AI marketing team. A brand enters its website; agents read it and draft five strategy documents (product, marketing strategy, competitors, brand voice, content strategy) that the team confirms. Specialist agents then audit SEO and AI visibility, find what to post, draft content, check it, and report results. People approve every outward action, tied to the exact version approved. Success: a new brand reaches confirmed strategy documents on day one, and the team publishes on-brand, accurate content weekly with measurable results.

## Brand Personality

Confident, warm, transparent. A capable colleague who shows its work: calm, specific language, no hype, no exclamation marks. Cinematic where it earns it (first-run, empty states, live agent activity), quiet everywhere else. It should feel crafted, like good furniture: solid, honest materials, nothing fake.

## Anti-references

- Okara's and Scalio's own logos, illustrations, copy or assets (reference their patterns, never copy them).
- Neon-green-on-black "AI tool" look; purple/blue AI gradients; sparkle-emoji AI branding.
- Generic SaaS landing templates: hero metric + three feature cards, gradient text, glassmorphism.
- Chatbot-first UIs where everything is a chat bubble.
- Dashboards that hide what the AI did or invent numbers.

## Design Principles

1. Agents draft, people decide: every AI output is visibly a draft until a person confirms or approves it.
2. Show the work: live activity, sources and open questions are always one glance away.
3. One brand at a time: the active brand is always clear; nothing mixes between brands.
4. Earned familiarity: standard product patterns for daily work; distinctive moments only where they help (first run, live agent activity).
5. Never invent: unknowns are shown as open questions, not filled with plausible text.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA in dark and light themes. Full keyboard use with visible focus. Reduced motion disables typing and pulse animations. Content is often French: leave room for longer strings and accented characters.
